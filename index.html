Research

Lab work comes first. Most of it sits around one group at USF.

Magnonics and magnetic materials

Research support for Dr. Arena's Magnetic Materials and Spin Dynamics Lab, which studies nanomagnetism, electronic structure, and spin dynamics with x-ray and neutron probes. My part is on the magnonics side: spin waves as a way to carry and process signals.

  • Spin dynamics
  • Spectroscopy
  • Nanomagnetism
Lab page ↗

Muon telescope

Simulation for a telescope that sorts cosmic-ray muons by charge. A magnetic field bends μ⁺ and μ⁻ in opposite directions, and an offset detector pair in coincidence keeps one sign. I'm moving the model from a Python Monte Carlo to a native Geant4 simulation.

  • Geant4
  • C++
  • Detector physics

MANTiS and x-ray spectromicroscopy

Packaging and workflow notes for MANTiS, the multivariate analysis toolkit for hyperspectral x-ray data: preprocessing, PCA, cluster analysis, spectral maps, and peak fitting. I also wrote a small converter between GIF stacks and MANTiS TIFF stacks with their energy lists.

  • Nix
  • Python
  • Hyperspectral data
